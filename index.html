VikerStudios Uploader

A small private tool that publishes solution videos for our maze game to the VikerStudios YouTube channel.

What it does

Each day it picks a few finished solution videos and uploads them to the channel as Shorts, so players who get stuck on a level can find an official answer.

Who uses it

Only the VikerStudios developer, signed in with the channel's own Google account. The tool has no public sign-in. Visitors to this page don't need an account and don't share any data with it.
